What ran

All 13,613 hosts from the same nodes.csv, with 32,768 validators and 2,400-byte signatures. Each of 16 committees had four aggregator hosts. Seeds change the generated placement, peering and proposer selection; they do not change the workload settings.

Signatures use bundled fixed-four Waggle with a 200 ms pull delay. Committee proving starts after 90% signature receipt and runs at a configured rate of 1,000 signatures/s. Each resulting 200,000-byte proof uses 64 authenticated Waggle chunks, with signed commitments over GossipSub. The proposer waits for one complete proof from each committee, models a 1.200 s combine, then propagates the global proof the same way.

Three sequential Shadow runs, each with 48 workers and a 500 GB cap with swap disabled. Time zero is the first signature send, not the start of Shadow. Percentiles use the nearest rank. Traffic is the total QUIC stream-write tally over the run, not IP wire bytes or a phase-specific counter.

Earlier attempt and scope

The original 300 GiB full-map attempt was killed by its memory cap after about 10 s of simulated time, before generating a global proof. It is not included in the comparison. The three successful runs measure one modeled pipeline; they do not establish a live-network slot bound or compare against another transport.
